# Robot summary, Oct 30 – Nov 4

Numbers are from the fitted MuJoCo sim at 65 Hz unless marked *robot*. Tracking is the correlation between commanded and measured forward speed.

- **Starting point** *(robot)*: the round-5 command policies wobbled and fell a lot. Tracking was 0.55 on carpet, and the table policy fell 39 times per minute.
- **Sim refit**: the sim was fitted to whole recorded runs instead of short windows. Gearbox play turned out to cause the wobble, and the sim's mismatch with the robot fell from 18 to 0.66.
- **Mixed-floor stick driving**: randomising the tilt-sensor lag plus the CAPS spatial smoothing term cut carpet falls about 20x. Pick: `r6_mixed` (e8261), tracking 0.92 on carpet and 0.86 on the table, about 0 falls per minute.
- **Mixed-floor balance**: pick `r6_bal_mixed` (e9671). It falls 45–60 times per minute if the true balance point is off by 1°.
- **One policy for stick and balance**: no fixed balance point, trained on slopes. `r6w_unified` and `r6v_unified` fall far less on slopes and with a shifted balance point. Tracking on normal floors drops to 0.49–0.70, and they still roll at zero stick when the balance point shifts. On the robot, `r6w_unified` barely followed the forward stick.
- **Robot from Germany**: the phone now connects to the Saion learner through a relay on the local computer. With int8 delta weights over 8 streams, the policy on the phone is 4–6 s old, down from 66–114 s.
- **Live-demo runaway on the table**: early in training the robot chases its own lean at full drive. Reward tweaks didn't remove it. A runaway brake (c2b7c00) cuts the worst minute from about 17% to 6% of the time.
- **Position leash** *(not committed)*: weight 0.3 halves early roaming on the table, but balancing comes about 2 min later. No effect on carpet.

**Use now:** `r6_mixed` for stick driving and `r6v_unified` for stick plus balance on slopes. Carpet is the reliable floor for the live demo.

**Open problems:** the policy rolls at zero stick when the balance point shifts, the unified policies track the stick worse than `r6_mixed`, the robot drifts about 2 m/min on the table after balancing, and results vary a lot with the seed.
