## 2. What was found

**"Colleague balances at 200 Hz with PID": it's the firmware.** Our RP2040 runs
firmware `main`, which does a full `get_state()` (charger, fuel gauge, ADCs,
~77 log calls) on every `SET_MOTOR_LEVEL` and sleeps 10 ms between USB polls.
That is the ~92 ms per command, i.e. 10.8 Hz. The maintainer fixed it on
2026-06-22 (`RTT-LoopReduction` commit 7bb9193: reply with encoder counts only,
drain up to 8 packets, 1 ms idle sleep), but it is **not merged to firmware
`main`**. The repo's own RTT benchmark quotes ~15 ms mean (≈50-120 Hz). The
Android half (no background `GET_STATE` polling) is already in `dreamer-bridge`.
The colleague's `pidBalancer` (tutorial branch) runs its loop at 200 Hz
(`setTimestep(5)`), but how many commands reach the wheels depends on the
firmware. **Not yet confirmed which firmware the colleague has.**

**Real-time cartpole sim (`sim_phone`).** One DMC env paced to wall-clock time
(`env.realtime_hz`, `wrappers.RealTime`) through the robot's actor/learner
split, with the actor playing the phone. 92 runs (e1113-e1196, e1215-e1222):

| config | min to hold 800 (cartpole swingup) |
|---|---|
| `sim_phone` defaults, 50 Hz | 25.5 (4 seeds) |
| **`sim_phone_fast`**: 12.5 Hz, horizon 2 s, lr 3e-4, MLP 256, batch 32×64 | **10.6** (4 seeds) |
| same knobs at 25 Hz | ~12.8 (3 seeds) |

What mattered: (1) never let the learner wait; (2) a ~2 s return horizon
instead of 333 steps; (3) at low control rates, lr 3e-4 + wider MLPs; (4) batch
32×64. What didn't: weight-push interval 5-60 s, imagination length, RSSM
size, entropy, MLP 512, and a full step of action delay (the recipe is robust
to it). **Caveat:** 12.5 Hz never learns reacher_easy; 25-50 Hz with the same
knobs does. **25 Hz is the safer rate for the robot.** Tuning has plateaued;
see figures in `docs/figs/` and `tools/plot_sim_sweep.py`.

**Literature (survey doc).** DayDreamer and A Walk in the Park both put a **PD
loop on the hardware** under a 20 Hz RL policy that outputs joint-angle
targets. DayDreamer uses discount 0.95, ≈1 s at 20 Hz, consistent with our
2 s. Our policy outputs wheel PWM directly, so it *is* the balance loop.

4. **First run on Saion**, from the Mac:
   ```bash
   ssh saion 'cd /apps/unit/DoyaU/vasilache/apps/code/robot/dreamerv3 && git pull && \
     CODE=$PWD SCRIPT=train CONFIGS="robot_daydreamer robot_fast" PORT=3000 \
     sbatch -J e<N>_robot --nodelist=saion-gpu20 sbatch/run_robot_v100.sbatch \
     --env.robot.onboard True --agent.horizon <2 s × Hz>'
   ```
   `robot_fast` (opt-in, untested on hardware) sets horizon 22 (2 s at
   10.8 Hz), lr 3e-4, batch 32×64, train_ratio 8192. **Override the horizon to
   match the real rate** (50 at 25 Hz). Drop `--nodelist`/`PORT` once (3a) is
   done. Watch `online_shared/policy/latest` advance.
5. **The bigger lever: a PID inner loop on the phone (or in the firmware),
   with Dreamer setting its setpoint** (target tilt or wheel speed) or adding a
   residual. This is DayDreamer/Walk-in-the-Park style, and most likely to
   beat the sim plateau. Also consider warm-starting from the colleague's
   simulator, and an A100 learner when the HRL b64 array frees one.

## 4. Traps (old and new)

- **Learner health = `online_shared/policy/latest` advancing.** Nothing else
  proves it.
- A crashed learner leaves `logdir/error_learner`, and the next job exits after
  ~30 s until it is deleted. Delete a stale `online_shared/actor_step` before
  resuming with a fresh actor.
- Pre-session phone checks (`ROBOT.md` §4d): `mWakefulness=Awake`,
  `SerialLatency` max, `dumpsys usb | grep source_power` = true. The phone was
  at ~18% battery on 2026-10-05; charge it, the base draws from it.
- `tools/fake_robot_phone.py` is stale (pacing/seq); no hardware-free test of
  the robot path. Use `sim_phone` for algorithm work.
- **Experiment numbers collide** with other sessions/arrays. Recheck max N in
  `EXPERIMENTS.md` (main checkout) and `/work` right before every submit, and
  glob full prefixes when archiving. Round 14 had to be renumbered
  e1197-e1204 → e1215-e1222.
- **Partial bucket copies:** that collision made the archive script copy 8
  *live* director_og pinpad runs (`e1197_pinpad_six…`–`e1204_pinpad_four…`,
  array 4744957) into the bucket at 11:14-11:17. Their `/work` dirs are
  untouched; their normal archiving overwrites the copies. The user has been
  told and may delete them.
- The Slurm controller (`saion-sched1`) went down twice on 2026-10-05. Submit
  scripts retry.
- `dreamerv3_env`'s matplotlib is broken (NumPy 2). Plots used a scratch venv
  with matplotlib on Python 3.11. **The user always wants plots**, in their

- The run log `EXPERIMENTS.md` lives in the **main** checkout
